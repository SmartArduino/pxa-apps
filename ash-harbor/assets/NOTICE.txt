ASH HARBOR / 烬港斗魂

Original character art, action frames, stage, title lettering and
combat visual effects and UI graphics were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for this
project. Original PNGs are preserved in art/source; prompts are recorded in
tools/art-prompts.json, tools/ui-prompts.json, tools/walk-prompt.json, tools/walk-finish-prompts.json and tools/expansion-prompts.json and tools/stage-control-prompts.json. UI sources are hashed in
art/ui-sources.json; expanded fighter, story and scenery sources in art/expansion-sources.json. Runtime rasters are deterministic silhouette crops and indexed
versions produced by tools/build_art.py.

Chinese labels and ordinary text use unmodified Fusion Pixel Font 12px monospaced zh_hans,
release 2026.11.10, copyright 2022 TakWolf, licensed under SIL OFL 1.1.
The original font and source metadata are in tools/fonts. Runtime glyphs are
native 12px rasterizations. Timer numerals and KO/FIGHT/ROUND/WIN announcements
use the original generated sprite artwork, separately from this font.
The complete font license and upstream notices are in assets/licenses/fusion-pixel.
Source: https://github.com/TakWolf/fusion-pixel-font

No third-party game character graphics, game data or recorded game audio are
included. Original music and effects were generated with the installed ACE-Step 1.5 and
Stable Audio 3 Small SFX models through the user's remote ComfyUI. Original character voices were
designed with Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-VoiceDesign (Apache-2.0). The selected casting
references are YAN take B, LAN take A, ZHEN take A and LING take B. Dialogue,
effort, pain and KO calls are performed with Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-Base (Apache-2.0),
conditioned on those synthetic references. Reference hashes and transcripts are
recorded in tools/voice-prompts.json. Complete performances are silence-trimmed
and stored as 16-bit PCM; no third-party human voice recordings are used.
Model source: https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-TTS
No model weights are distributed. Prompts, selected source
hashes and conversion settings are in tools/audio-prompts.json and
tools/voice-prompts.json and tools/scene-music-prompts.json. Selected FLAC masters are preserved in art/audio.
The MIT-licensed common/motion-games/sound.hpp provides audio lifecycle and
permission handling.

SNK's published command list and manual were consulted only as design references
for directional inputs, guarding and cancels. No source code or audiovisual
assets from SNK or Capcom were imported.
