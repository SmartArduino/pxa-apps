OpenLara engine: https://github.com/XProger/OpenLara

Engine source is pinned to 3b268ca4bf986c380f44a26ffd9218bf6d376249.
Prepared media is separate from the engine license below. The default test
media uses the public PC demo TITLE/LEVEL2 from LostArtefacts/TRX-data at
121815b61ae16884a6f775dc0966173cea5e891f, plus the pinned OpenLara GBA title
art/palette and AD4 bank. It contains one campaign level, not the retail game.
Imported original game files are supplied separately by the user. Consult
game/data-manifest.json for hashes, installed levels and missing data.
