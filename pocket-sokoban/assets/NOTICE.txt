Player.ino and LifoBuffer.ino movement/rewind rules were adapted to a checked
10x10 dual-layer state with static circular undo, PXA input/render hooks,
progress counters and validated persistence. Arduino animation and audio were
removed. No original NibbleSokoban levels or graphical assets are included.

to C++ string arrays without changing their 10x10 geometry. Original dataset
IDs and SHA-256 checksums are in SOURCES.json. Two additional tutorial boards
are original CC0 works.
