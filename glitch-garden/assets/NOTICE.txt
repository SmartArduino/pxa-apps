露芽守卫 / Dewbud Guard

PXA game code, rendering adapter, build tools, and procedural sound effects:
MIT. Full license: LICENSE-PXA-MIT.txt.

Original scene, character sheets, UI art, store cover, title/button lettering and glyph atlas:
generated with the built-in image_gen tool for this project from original
prompts. The complete prompts are in art/prompts.json in the source release.
To the extent any copyrights or related rights are held by the contributors,
these original art assets are dedicated under CC0 1.0 Universal; full dedication
is in CC0-ART.txt. No exclusivity or copyrightability of AI output is asserted.
They may be copied, modified and redistributed with or separately from this game.

No artwork, fonts, music, character likenesses or logos from Plants vs. Zombies
or Glitch are included. The game does not require the original games or Unity.
The generated lettering is a fixed game UI phrase/glyph collection, not a
complete general-purpose Unicode font. No system/common font is requested by
the game's renderer.
