=== FONT-NOTICE ===

PXA Sans SC Regular (SIL Open Font License 1.1)

Device profile: zh-Hans,en plus study phonetics and poetry characters (2026-10-29).
Covers all 8,105 characters in the 2013 Table of General Standard Chinese
Characters (including 196 supplementary-plane Han characters), printable
English ASCII, Pinyin tone letters, common English IPA, rare poetry characters 罍、殽、晞、曈、浥、焜, and the existing
system punctuation, symbols and icon mappings.
Unselected traditional-only, Japanese, Korean and other writing systems are
not included. Shared simplified/traditional characters remain present.

Based on the previously bundled Noto Sans SC subset. Selected existing
outlines and metrics are preserved; additional mainland sans-serif glyphs
come from Noto Sans CJK SC Regular 2.004, TTC face 2. The derivative's internal
family is PXA Sans SC. Its legacy filename is kept for existing firmware paths.
This is not a complete Unicode font. Unknown characters have an outlined
.notdef glyph. No additional runtime font face or fallback chain is required.

Noto Sans CJK copyright: 2014-2021 Adobe (http://www.adobe.com/).
Noto is a trademark of Google Inc. Distribute this NOTICE and OFL.txt with
this font. Latin phonetic outlines also use Noto Sans Regular 2.015:
Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors (https://github.com/notofonts/latin-greek-cyrillic).
Its SIL Open Font License 1.1 is preserved in OFL-Latin.txt. Both licenses
and this NOTICE must accompany the derivative. Build-time fontTools is
not needed on the device.

Build tool: tools/fonts/build_common_font.py --traditional none
 --corpus tools/fonts/study-phonetics.txt --corpus tools/fonts/study-hanzi.txt
 --fallback-source NotoSans-Regular.ttf
